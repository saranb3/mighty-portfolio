<CaseStudySection heading="Overview">

For the 2025–26 school year I was president of the Thai Student Association at UIUC — a 100+ member org that's home base for Thai and Thai-curious students a long way from home. I owned operations end to end: the calendar, the exec team, the budget, and the twelve events we shipped, from **Songkran on the Quad** to the **Welcome Dinner**.

</CaseStudySection>

<CaseStudySection heading="Challenges">

A student org runs on volunteers who are also full-time students, and that makes three things genuinely hard:

- **Momentum** — people disappear at midterms, and events lose steam.
- **Money** — programming costs more than membership dues bring in.
- **Memory** — the board turns over every year, and institutional knowledge walks out the door with it.

</CaseStudySection>

<CaseStudySection heading="Solutions">

- **Momentum:** weekly exec standups and a calendar planned a semester ahead, so nothing depended on a last-minute scramble.
- **Money:** a Thai Tea Sale and small fundraisers covered the gap — **$409 raised** — which kept ticket prices low.
- **Memory:** a freshman mentor program with four rotating *P'Nong* pairs, plus written run-of-show docs so next year's board starts with a playbook instead of a blank page.
